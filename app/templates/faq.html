General

Zirel is a worldbuilding logic tool that helps writers, game masters, and storytellers organize fictional worlds and detect possible logic issues.

Zirel is for writers, D&D and game masters, screenwriters, indie game developers, and anyone building fictional worlds with characters, factions, events, and relationships.

No. Zirel is not a story generator. It helps organize and analyze the structure of a fictional world.

Notion and Obsidian are flexible note-taking tools. Zirel is focused specifically on worldbuilding logic, relationship graphs, and consistency warnings.

Data & ownership

You do. Zirel does not claim ownership over your fictional worlds, characters, factions, events, notes, relationships, or story ideas.

Yes. Zirel supports JSON export so you can download your project data.

Yes. Zirel can import JSON files exported from Zirel.

During the MVP and beta stage, data deletion tools may still be limited. You can contact the project owner for deletion requests. More self-service deletion options may be added later.

Beta & safety

No. Zirel is currently an early MVP and beta. Features, design, and workflows may change.

During beta, users should avoid uploading highly sensitive, confidential, or irreplaceable writing material. Use test worlds or backups when possible.
