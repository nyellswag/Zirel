Mostly completed / in progress

Q1 - MVP Foundation

Build the core product foundation and validate the basic worldbuilding workflow.

  • Project creation and management
  • Characters, factions, and events
  • Relationship builder
  • Logic warnings
  • Graph workspace
  • Export and import JSON
  • User accounts and protected projects
  • Admin dashboard
  • Legal, FAQ, beta, and feedback pages
Planned

Q2 - Beta Testing & UX Improvements

Prepare Zirel for early testers and improve the core user experience.

  • Closed beta testing
  • Better onboarding
  • Improved project dashboard
  • More polished graph workspace
  • Better warning explanations
  • Search and filter improvements
  • Data deletion tools
  • User settings
  • Style system and full redesign pass
Exploration

Q3 - AI Assistance & Advanced World Tools

Explore AI-assisted workflows and deeper worldbuilding analysis.

  • AI scene analysis prototype
  • Extract possible facts from text
  • Suggest characters, events, and relations
  • User review before saving AI suggestions
  • Advanced consistency checks
  • Warning overlays on graph
  • Export improvements
  • Collaboration prototype
Future direction

Q4 - Public Launch & Monetization Research

Prepare Zirel for a broader public release and evaluate sustainable support models.

  • Public beta or launch preparation
  • Pricing experiments
  • Support or donation options
  • Possible paid plans
  • Team workspace research
  • Community launch planning
  • Product Hunt and writing community outreach
  • Trademark and IP review
Note

What may change

Roadmap items may move, be removed, or be replaced based on beta feedback, technical constraints, and user needs. This is a guide to current priorities, not a guarantee of delivery dates or specific features.
