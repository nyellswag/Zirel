Known limitations

  • User accounts are still basic.
  • No cloud sync yet.
  • No AI analysis yet.
  • Logic warnings are rule-based and may miss issues.
  • Design and UX are still evolving.
04

Data safety note

This is an early MVP. Please avoid highly sensitive, confidential, or irreplaceable writing material during beta testing.

You can export project data as JSON from the project dashboard.
