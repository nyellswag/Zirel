Legal Center

These documents explain how Zirel handles user content, privacy, beta usage, and responsibility.

This is an early MVP legal draft and not legal advice.

Terms of Service Privacy Policy Disclaimer Content Ownership Data Export & Deletion Beta Notice Future AI Policy Future Refund Policy Contact

Terms of Service

  • Use of the service: You may use Zirel to organize fictional worlds, entities, timelines, and relationships, review logic warnings, explore graph views, and export project data.
  • User responsibility: You are responsible for the content you enter and for the final creative, legal, publishing, and commercial decisions connected to your work.
  • Prohibited use: Do not use Zirel to store unlawful material, infringe others' rights, interfere with the service, or attempt to misuse app data or accounts.
  • MVP and beta status: Zirel is an early MVP. Features may change, break, be removed, or behave unexpectedly while the product evolves.
  • Changes to the service: We may update, change, or discontinue parts of Zirel and these draft policies as the beta develops.

Privacy Policy

  • Data we may store: The MVP may store account information, project names and descriptions, and the entities and relationships you create.
  • Project content: Project content can include characters, factions, events, descriptions, relations, and dynamically generated logic warnings.
  • Feedback and contact messages: Feedback and contact forms may store the name, email address, selected category, and message you submit.
  • Account data: Accounts store a username, email address, password hash, role, admin status, and account creation date. Passwords are not stored in plain text.
  • Data export: You can export project data as JSON. Exports help you keep a local copy and move your project data between supported Zirel workflows.
  • Future AI data notice: Any future AI feature will include clearer information about what text is processed and how that processing works.

Disclaimer

  • Creative assistance only: Zirel is a creative organization and review tool. It does not make final creative decisions for you.
  • Logic warnings are suggestions: Warnings identify possible inconsistencies based on recorded project data. They are prompts for review, not definitive conclusions.
  • No guarantee of perfect accuracy: Zirel does not guarantee that a world, book, game, or screenplay is complete, accurate, publishable, or error-free.
  • Your decisions remain yours: You remain responsible for creative, legal, publishing, and commercial decisions. Seek appropriate professional advice when needed.

Content Ownership

  • Users keep ownership of their fictional worlds, characters, factions, events, stories, notes, relationships, and other creative content.
  • Zirel does not claim ownership over user-created content.
  • Users grant Zirel limited permission to store, display, process, analyze, and export their content only to provide the service.

Data Export & Deletion

  • Projects can be exported as JSON, helping with backups and portability.
  • Import and export are intended to give users more control over their work.
  • Future account and data deletion tools may be added.
  • During beta, users may contact the project owner with a deletion request.

Beta Notice

  • Zirel is an early MVP/beta.
  • Features may change, and bugs or data loss are possible in early testing.
  • Users should avoid adding highly sensitive, confidential, or irreplaceable material during beta.
  • Users should export backups of projects they want to keep.

Future AI Policy

  • AI features are not active unless they are clearly added in a future release.
  • If introduced, users will receive clearer information about how text is processed.
  • AI outputs may be inaccurate and should always be reviewed by the user.

Future Refund Policy

  • Zirel is not currently accepting payments through this site.
  • If paid features, donations, or subscriptions are added later, clear payment and refund terms will be provided before they take effect.

Contact

  • Questions about these draft policies or a beta deletion request can be sent through the Contact page.
