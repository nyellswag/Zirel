Introduction

These Terms of Service describe the basic rules for using Zirel, an early MVP workspace for writers, game masters, and worldbuilders.

By using Zirel, you agree to use the service responsibly and understand that the product is still evolving.

Early MVP draft

This document is informational and not legal advice. Final public launch terms may change.

02

Use of the Service.

You may use Zirel to create projects, organize fictional entities, connect relationships, review logic warnings, explore graph views, and export project data.

You are responsible for the information you enter and for how you use the output of the app.

03

User Content

User content includes the fictional worlds, characters, factions, events, stories, notes, descriptions, relationships, and other material you enter into Zirel.

You should avoid adding highly sensitive, confidential, or irreplaceable writing material during the early beta stage.

04

Content Ownership

You keep ownership of your fictional worlds, characters, factions, events, stories, notes, and relationships.

Zirel does not claim ownership over user-created content.

By using the service, you grant Zirel limited permission to store, display, process, analyze, and export your content only as needed to provide the service.

05

Prohibited Use

You should not use Zirel to store unlawful material, infringe on others' rights, attack the service, interfere with other users, or attempt to misuse the app or its data.

You should not attempt to bypass route protection, access another user's private data, or disrupt the service.

06

Beta / MVP Notice

Zirel is an early MVP. Features may change, break, be removed, or behave unexpectedly while the product is being developed.

Users should keep backups of important projects by using JSON export.

07

No Guarantee of Perfect Accuracy

Zirel may help identify possible contradictions, but it does not guarantee that a fictional world, story, book, game, or screenplay is complete, accurate, or error-free.

Logic warnings are suggestions for review and may miss issues or flag intentional story choices.

08

Changes to the Service

We may update, change, or discontinue parts of Zirel as the MVP evolves.

Future versions may include new features, notices, or terms.

09

Contact

For questions about these terms, contact the Zirel project owner or maintainer through the Contact page.
