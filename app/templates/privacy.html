01

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains the basic types of data Zirel may store or process while helping users organize fictional worldbuilding projects.

The policy may change as hosting, accounts, collaboration, payments, or AI features are added later.

Early MVP draft

This page is a plain-language draft for beta use and is not legal advice.

02

Data We May Store

The current MVP may store project data locally or in the app database.

This may include project names, descriptions, characters, factions, events, relations, and related notes.

Account data may include username, email address, password hash, role, admin status, and creation date.

03

Project Content

Project data may include characters, factions, events, relations, descriptions, and dynamically generated logic warnings.

Logic warnings are generated from your project content when viewed or exported and are not currently stored as separate warning records.

04

Usage Data

Future versions may collect limited usage information to understand which features are used and to improve reliability.

This MVP does not currently include payments or payment-processing data.

05

Data Export

You can export project data using the JSON export feature.

Exports may include project details, entities, relations, and current logic warnings.

Export/import is intended to support backups and portability.

06

Data Deletion

You can delete projects and related project data from within the app.

Deleting a project removes its associated characters, factions, events, and relations from the app database.

During beta, you may contact the project owner for broader deletion questions or requests.

07

AI Features Notice for the Future

Zirel does not add AI functionality in this MVP unless clearly enabled in a later release.

Future AI features may require a separate AI data notice explaining what content is sent to AI systems and how it is processed.

08

Data Security

We aim to handle project data responsibly, but this is an early MVP and no system can be guaranteed perfectly secure.

Avoid storing highly sensitive personal information or irreplaceable writing material in Zirel during beta.

09

Changes to this Policy

This policy may be updated as Zirel grows, especially if hosting, accounts, collaboration, payments, or AI features are added.

10

Contact

For questions about privacy, contact the Zirel project owner or maintainer through the Contact page.
